Questions & Answers

Do I have to live in Pennsylvania to use Keystone Remote Notary?

No. While our notary public is physically located and commissioned in Pennsylvania, Pennsylvania law (57 Pa.C.S. Chapter 3) authorizes us to perform remote online notarizations for signers located anywhere in the United States—and in many cases internationally—for documents within U.S. jurisdiction. Under the Full Faith and Credit Clause and state interstate recognition laws, a valid Pennsylvania remote notarization is recognized across all 50 states.

How does the BlueNotary appointment and payment process work?

You start right here on our website—either through the fee estimator or by chatting with KATE. Once we confirm your document readiness and appointment time, we send you a personalized BlueNotary video session invitation link by email. BlueNotary guides you through identity verification and hosts the encrypted video signing room with your Pennsylvania notary.

Can any employer use remote video inspection for Form I-9?

Under Department of Homeland Security regulations (88 FR 47990), remote video examination of Form I-9 identity documents is only permitted under the DHS Alternative Procedure if the hiring employer is enrolled in good standing in E-Verify. For employers not enrolled in E-Verify, Form I-9 Section 2 requires physical, in-person examination of original documents by an Authorized Representative at the employee's location.

What documents qualify for Pennsylvania Apostille facilitation?

The Pennsylvania Department of State in Harrisburg can only issue apostilles for documents issued by Pennsylvania state or county officials (such as birth, marriage, or death certificates and court records) or documents properly notarized by a commissioned Pennsylvania notary public. If you have an eligible document (such as a power of attorney or affidavit), we can notarize it remotely first and then coordinate Harrisburg apostille issuance.
